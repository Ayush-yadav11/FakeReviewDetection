    Does it catch reviews written by ChatGPT?

    Not reliably. It learned from reviews GPT-2 wrote for this dataset, so text from newer models can score as original.

    Does a review I paste leave my browser?

    No. The page downloads the 0.39 MB model once and scores in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Why does the detector use logistic regression?

    The SVC is 1.5 points better, but logistic regression fits in one small file and explains its score word by word.

    Can I train the detector myself?

    Yes: pip install -r requirements.txt, then python train.py. All three models take about six minutes on a laptop.

    What do the CG and OR review labels mean?

    CG marks a computer-generated review and OR an original one written by a person.
